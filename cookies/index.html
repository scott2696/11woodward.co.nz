What cookies are, the types we use — essential, analytics and affiliate tracking — and how you can control or switch them off at any time.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They let a site remember your actions and preferences over time, and they help the site work properly and understand how it's being used. Similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels and tracking tags — do comparable things, and where we say "cookies" in this policy we mean all of them.

Cookies can be "session" cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser, or "persistent" cookies, which stay on your device for a set period. They can be set by us ("first-party") or by a third-party service we use ("third-party").

Consent

When you first visit the Site, we ask for your consent to non-essential cookies (analytics and affiliate tracking) through a cookie banner or consent tool. Essential cookies do not require consent because the Site cannot work without them. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies, and you can change your choice at any time using the controls described below. Declining non-essential cookies will not stop you from reading any of our content.

How to manage or delete cookies

You are always in control. You can manage cookies in two ways:

  • Through our consent tool — reopen the cookie settings on the Site to change which non-essential cookies you allow.
  • Through your browser — every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. You can usually find these controls under Settings → Privacy. Helpful guides are available for Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.

You can also browse in your browser's private/incognito mode, which typically clears cookies at the end of the session. Note that blocking all cookies may affect how some parts of the Site behave.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we rely on, such as our analytics provider, and by operators you click through to. When you leave our Site for a third-party operator, we encourage you to review that operator's own cookie and privacy notices.

For more on how we handle the data collected through cookies, see our Privacy Policy.
