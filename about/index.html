About 11 Woodward: Our Kiwi Casino Review Team

We're an independent New Zealand team that tests real money pokie sites the way Kiwis actually use them — depositing in NZD, timing withdrawals, and reading every bonus term before we recommend anything.

Our mission

11 Woodward exists to help everyday Kiwis find online pokie sites that pay fast and play fair. New Zealanders are shown some of the loudest bonus offers in the world, and it is genuinely hard to tell a site that will pay you within a day from one that will hold your winnings for a week and hope you gamble them back. We do that legwork so you don't have to.

We are not owned by a casino operator and we do not run any gambling accounts. Our job is to compare sites on the things that actually matter to a New Zealand player — how quickly money lands back in an NZD bank account or wallet, whether the site holds your balance in New Zealand dollars, and whether the welcome bonus is worth anything once you read the wagering terms in full.

How we test pokie sites

Star ratings are easy to fake, so we anchor ours to things we can measure with our own money and our own stopwatch. Every site we review goes through the same hands-on process:

💳 We deposit in NZD

We fund a real account in New Zealand dollars where possible, and note the exact conversion spread when a site forces AUD, USD or EUR at deposit.

⏱ We time every withdrawal

We win, request a cashout, and record how long it takes to reach a NZ bank account, e-wallet or crypto wallet — measured in real New Zealand business days.

📄 We read the bonus terms in full

Wagering multiplier, max bet, game weighting, max cashout and expiry all get read line by line, so we can tell you what a bonus is really worth after wagering.

🔒 We check licensing & KYC

We verify the operator's licence, complete identity verification (KYC), and note where an operator is preparing for the new NZ licensing regime.

We also run a small test cashout on every site — how a casino handles a $20 withdrawal usually tells you exactly how it will handle a $2,000 one. If support goes quiet, terms change without notice, or a payout is stalled without reason, that shows up in the rating.

Editorial independence

Our rankings are editorial and are decided by our reviewers, not by commercial partners. We keep our testing methodology and our commercial relationships strictly separate — a partnership can never buy a higher position on any table. If a site pays fast, holds NZD, and treats players fairly, it ranks well; if it doesn't, no arrangement changes that.

Where an operator falls short on a specific point — slow payouts, punishing wagering, or poor support — we say so plainly, even for well-known brands. Our long-term value to Kiwi readers depends entirely on giving you an honest read, and we protect that above everything else.

Quarterly re-testing

A site that paid fast in January can quietly change its withdrawal policy by April. To keep our recommendations honest, we re-test every operator on our lists at least once a quarter, repeating the deposit, play and cashout cycle and re-reading the current terms.

What we re-check each quarterWhy it matters to Kiwis
Payout timesThe single biggest reason a site rises or falls on our tables.
NZD banking & payment methodsSites drop or add local methods; conversion fees change.
Current bonus termsWagering and max-bet rules are quietly revised more often than you'd think.
Licensing statusWe track which operators are applying under the new NZ regime.
Support responsivenessWe re-open a live chat and time the real response.

Any operator that slows payouts, changes bonus terms without notice, or starts ignoring support tickets gets marked down or dropped entirely. The "last updated" date on each page tells you when we last verified it.
