The New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and the 13 Information Privacy Principles

We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020, which is New Zealand's main data protection law and is overseen by the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC). The Act sets out 13 Information Privacy Principles (IPPs) that govern how personal information may be collected, used, stored, disclosed and accessed. In summary, the principles require us to:

IPPWhat it requires
IPP 1–4Only collect personal information we genuinely need, for a lawful purpose, directly from you where reasonable, and in a fair, lawful and non-intrusive way.
IPP 5Keep personal information secure and protect it against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.
IPP 6Give you the right to access the personal information we hold about you.
IPP 7Give you the right to request correction of that information.
IPP 8Check information is accurate, complete and up to date before we use it.
IPP 9Not keep personal information for longer than we need it.
IPP 10–11Use and disclose personal information only for the purpose it was collected, except in limited permitted circumstances.
IPP 12Take care before any information is disclosed outside New Zealand, ensuring comparable protection.
IPP 13Limit how unique identifiers are assigned and used.

What personal information we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. Depending on how you use the Site, we may collect:

  • Information you give us directly — for example your name, email address, subject and message when you complete our contact form or email us.
  • Technical and usage data collected automatically — such as your IP address, approximate location, browser type and version, device type, the pages you view, the links you click, referring URL, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Cookie and analytics data — information gathered through cookies and similar technologies, as described in our Cookie Policy.

We do not ask for, and you should never send us, passwords, payment card numbers, bank details or copies of identity documents. We are not a casino and never need them.

Why we collect and use it

We only use personal information for the purposes for which it was collected, including to:

  • respond to your enquiries, feedback and correction requests;
  • operate, secure and maintain the Site and prevent misuse or fraud;
  • understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve our content and layout;
  • measure the performance of our pages and outbound links; and
  • comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

Cookies and analytics

The Site uses cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, to measure traffic through analytics tools, and to track referrals to third-party operators. Analytics providers may process aggregated, largely non-identifying usage data on our behalf. You can control or refuse cookies through your browser and our consent tools. For full detail on the types of cookies we use and how to manage them, see our Cookie Policy.

Sharing, storage and security

We may share personal information with trusted service providers who help us run the Site — for example hosting, email and analytics providers — strictly so they can perform those services for us. Some of these providers may store or process data outside New Zealand; where that happens, we take reasonable steps, consistent with IPP 12, to ensure your information receives comparable protection. We may also disclose information where required by law.

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected or to meet legal requirements, and then delete or de-identify it. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, in line with IPP 5. No online service can be completely secure, but we take our obligations seriously.

Please note that when you follow a link to a third-party operator, that operator collects and handles your data under its own privacy policy, over which we have no control.

Your rights to access and correct your data

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you (IPP 6);
  • Request correction of information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete or misleading (IPP 7); and
  • Ask us to delete information we no longer need to hold.

To make an access or correction request, email hello@11woodward.co.nz or use our contact page. We will respond as soon as reasonably practicable, and within the timeframes required by the Act. We may need to verify your identity before releasing information.
